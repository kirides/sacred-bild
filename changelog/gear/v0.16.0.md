## v0.16.0 (2026-11-28)

- New: `gear.inv.define_type(type, base, name_text)` makes an item type of a mod's own (ids 6150 to 6199) from a copy of a game item type, with a name of its own. A potion copy works with the `item.use` event; the Town Portal mod uses it.
- New: with SacredBild's NVRHI renderer, mod windows are drawn directly by SacredBild instead of through Direct3D 7 calls (needs SacredBild 0.4.0).
- Fixed: loading a savegame from the menu after leaving a game was refused with "a save or load is pending".
- Fixed: a refused load or delete now shows its real reason instead of always the slot message.
